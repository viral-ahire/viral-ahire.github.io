About

Building platforms that scale and stay secure

I have spent more than two decades building and operating infrastructure across public, private, and hybrid clouds. At Adobe, I design and run shared automation platforms used by more than 100 internal engineering teams, with a focus on multi-tenant governance, tenant isolation, and secrets management.

Alongside that work, I research how private clouds can match the elasticity of public clouds without giving up control. My work on two-level autoscaling across virtual and bare-metal infrastructure was presented at the IEEE International Conference on Cloud Engineering (IC2E 2026), and I am writing a book on multi-cloud infrastructure engineering for Apress.
